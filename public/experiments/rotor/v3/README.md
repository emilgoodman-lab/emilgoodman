# Rotor III — Octagonal Resonator

The outer path is a regular octagon with equal edges and a fixed center, plane and size. Motifs travel around the polygon perimeter; its support never rotates, tilts, drifts, sinks or vibrates. Per-ornament local X/Y plane transforms and tangent/Z rotation remain active. The outer ring is composited after kaleidoscope so mirror settings cannot alter its eight-sided support. The whole boundary fits the viewport.

Inner paths continuously blend from the polygon to a circle with u^2.4. Motif IDs remain permanent. Individual ring asset sizes include exact 0.5x and 2x versions of the preceding edition; counts adjust to preserve overlapping coverage. The original painted textures, shadows, minimal webcam UI and instrument audio are shared with v2.

Held animation now follows the original Rotor fields with a smoothed 1→2 speed/amplitude gain, replacing v2's much stronger independent stress drift/tilt/spin. Original stress displacement .009 becomes .018 and local shake .1 becomes .2. The fixed boundary is exempt from global spatial deformation.

Validation: regular octagon edges/vertices and circular center; every outer motif remains on its fixed support with zero depth under all fields and holds; clock/phase capped at 2x; permanent half/double asset scales; finite mobile projections and reliable release. Browser synthetic hand/touch, pause, mirror, audio-start and mobile checks. Hardware webcam testing is unchanged and not physically exercised.
