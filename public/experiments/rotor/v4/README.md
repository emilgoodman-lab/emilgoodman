# Rotor IV — Ten Rings

A new preserved edition with exactly ten permanent motifs sampled from Rotor II's four painted references. All four visual field weights start at their maximum of one. Without pointer/hand input they remain at maximum, with a 2× base motion clock and displacement/angle gain. Steering still blends the original cardinal fields; the fixed octagonal outer support remains exempt from global deformation. Held input retains a further relative increase and inversion. Half/double asset-scale variety is retained.

Every ornament is drawn at alpha 1, regardless of depth. Only cutout edges and baked paper shadows retain their natural alpha; there is no layer-opacity control or distance fade.

Compact English retro UI returns: sound/mute, volume, webcam, pause, kaleidoscope slider and a small state readout. The canvas has reserved space above/below so controls cannot cover the outer border. Painted imagery and the modulated acoustic score are shared with v2. Original webcam processing remains local.

Tests verify exactly ten distinct identities, maximum idle weights, double baseline clock, full opacity and finite positions under every field/hold, and fixed outer geometry at mobile size. Browser checks cover synthetic hand/touch/release, pause, mirror, audio activation and sound mute/resume, and responsive control layout. Webcam hardware was not physically exercised.

Catalog rotor selects v4; v1–v3 remain available at their original paths.
