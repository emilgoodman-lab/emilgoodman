# Talisman II — Paper Arcades

Preserved original Abyssal simulation, now between two cached paper scenery planes. Pale scalloped niches sit behind the creature. Hollow foreground arcades and hanging geometric ornaments cover passing body parts; clear openings show the creature. The user-reference texture remains shared with v2. Scenery is rebuilt only on resize.

Head component identity, order, mounting points and scale are permanent. The previous renderer multiplied absolute time by a mode-dependent agitation factor, causing discontinuous angle jumps. The new head uses small bounded oscillations with smoothly eased energy and no orbiting components or mode-dependent angle resets.

The procedural soundtrack replaces the electronic drone with physical-model plucked strings, a breath-flute-like voice and soft frame-drum-like percussion in a short room. Melodies are Turkish-inspired original material, not authentic historical recordings or exact traditional tuning. Capture increases ornament density; hunting produces short upper-register plucks. Sound begins on the first pointer press or webcam gesture. P pauses, M mutes. Only Enable webcam remains visible.

Validation: original physics and catalog tests; a 36,000-frame test verifies permanent head identities and continuous poses through repeated state changes. Browser checks cover capture, release, tracking loss, synthetic touch cancellation, pause/resume, mobile dimensions and reduced-motion start. Layer compositing checks confirm foreground occlusion and over 91% transparent openings at desktop/mobile sizes. Three actual audio graphs render finite stereo signals below clipping. Physical webcam hardware has not been tested in this edition.

The stable talisman catalog entry now selects v3. v2 remains available at its original URL.
